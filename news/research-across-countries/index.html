Ghana site visit

SFA delegation and NEPS team engage KNUST College of Health Sciences during Ghana visit

During a three-day Ghana visit, SFA and NEPS representatives met university leaders and visited local education and counselling partners to discuss implementation.

Ghana site visit

2 July 2026 · Source: University Relations Office

A delegation from the Science for Africa Foundation (SFA), together with researchers from the Navigating Educational Pressures and Stressors (NEPS) Project, paid a courtesy call on the Provost of the College of Health Sciences at Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), Kumasi, during a three-day Ghana site visit.

The visit formed part of SFA’s engagement with the NEPS team to review implementation progress, alignment with project objectives and institutional collaboration. NEPS is a 42-month initiative under SFA’s BEING Initiative, implemented in Ghana, Sierra Leone and Tanzania. It examines how educational pressures, poverty, stigma and other social stressors shape young people’s mental health and aims to inform practical support.

The delegation, which travelled from Kenya, met with the research team and university leadership to discuss operational and financial progress. SFA Programme Manager Byron Bitanihirwe said the Foundation would compile a report to guide the next phase and encouraged continued communication between the partners.

NEPS Principal Investigator Prof. Obed Brew said the review included project progress, financial management and implementation activities. Ghana Lead Prof. Daniel Ansong described the engagement as encouragement for the team. KNUST College of Health Sciences Provost Prof. Christian Agyare reaffirmed the university’s commitment and stressed the importance of extending project outcomes beyond the grant period.

The visit also included a courtesy call on the Kumasi Metropolitan Director of Education, an engagement with students at St. Louis Senior High School, and discussions with the KNUST Counselling Centre about its contribution and plans for a Mental Health Training Manual.
