Creative Art Therapy · WP6

Making space for expression, reflection and possibility.

A six-session intervention pathway uses creative expression, storytelling, drama, reflection and safe group processes. It aims to support emotional awareness, self-expression, coping, confidence and stigma reduction. These are aims to be evaluated, not established NEPS outcomes. [4]

Programme note

Delivery requires trained facilitators, appropriate safeguarding, agreed referral pathways and the relevant consent arrangements. The proposed co-deployment design treats this as an optional second-stage pathway, separately consented and evaluated. [3]

Six sessions · A supported journey

The session structure

  1. 01

    Orientation and Foundations of Expression

    Introduce the group, establish shared agreements and explore different ways of expressing feelings.

  2. 02

    Story of Me / Naming My Pain

    Use supported storytelling and reflection to explore personal experiences, with choice about what to share.

  3. 03

    Breaking the Box

    Explore labels, expectations and self-expression through creative activities and facilitated discussion.

  4. 04

    Finding My Power

    Reflect on strengths, coping resources and sources of support.

  5. 05

    Raise Your Voice

    Create space for expression and confidence, with voluntary participation and respect for boundaries.

  6. 06

    To My Future Self

    Reflect on learning, consider future hopes and close the group with support and referral information.

Choice, privacy and safety

Sharing is voluntary. Creative work and personal stories should not be displayed, recorded or published without appropriate permission. Participation is not a substitute for professional support when needed.
