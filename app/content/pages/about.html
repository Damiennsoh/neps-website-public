About NEPS

Youth wellbeing. A shared responsibility.

Navigating Educational Pressures and Stressors is a multi-country youth mental-health research programme across Ghana, Sierra Leone and Tanzania.

01

Why NEPS exists

NEPS examines educational pressure, poverty and mental-health-related stigma together, rather than treating each as an isolated issue. Its research asks how these experiences interact with schools, families, communities and available support. [1]

02

Our overall aim

The programme aims to generate evidence that can inform practical, context-sensitive responses to youth mental-health needs. Research, participation and policy engagement connect understanding with potential action. These are intended contributions, not claims of demonstrated impact. [1]

03

Young people aged 12–24

NEPS focuses on adolescents and young people aged 12–24. Eligibility for individual studies follows the relevant country protocols and consent arrangements; this page is not a recruitment invitation.

04

A consortium approach

A multidisciplinary consortium connects country teams, work package teams and scientific, community and youth perspectives. Programme leadership, country Co-PIs and coordinators, work package leads and management personnel are listed on the team and governance pages. Advisory board membership and remaining appointments will be added when confirmed.

05

What change could look like

NEPS seeks to contribute to clearer understanding of stress pathways, stronger mental-health literacy, safer support and referral, and evidence-informed decisions in schools and communities. Evaluation and implementation learning will determine what can responsibly be recommended. [1]

Wider evidence

WHO describes supportive family, school and community environments as important for adolescent mental health, and identifies poverty, stigma and barriers to care among relevant concerns. This wider evidence provides context; it is not a finding from NEPS. [5]
