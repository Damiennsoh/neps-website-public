Data, ethics & safeguarding

Trust is part of the research.

Respect, voluntary participation, privacy and participant protection guide the programme’s approach. Country-specific arrangements and the scope of each approved protocol govern research activities. [1,3]

Programme note

A public summary of country approvals, reference numbers, safeguarding contacts and referral routes is awaiting confirmation. This does not imply that ethics approvals or institutional permissions are absent.

01

Informed consent and assent

Participants receive age-appropriate information and an opportunity to decide freely. Consent, guardian permission and assent follow the applicable approved protocol. Participation and withdrawal arrangements must be explained without pressure.

02

Safeguarding and child protection

Delivery requires trained personnel, clear roles and a process for responding to concerns. Confidentiality has limits where protection requires escalation; those limits should be explained before participation.

03

Referral pathways

Country teams must confirm appropriate support and escalation routes before activities begin. A research website is not an emergency service or a substitute for local safeguarding procedures.

04

Confidentiality and data minimisation

Collect only information necessary for the approved purpose. Keep identifying information separate where appropriate, minimise exposure in reporting, and protect young people’s stories and creative work.

05

Role-based access and secure handling

Access should follow authorised responsibilities. Secure collection, storage, transfer and retention arrangements require documented institutional oversight. No participant-level data are provided on this website.

06

Cross-country governance

Data sharing and analysis must respect country approvals, institutional agreements and approved access arrangements. A public website does not confer permission to access research data.

07

Responsible digital tools and AI

Any proposed digital or AI use requires purpose-specific ethical, safeguarding and data-governance review. This site does not offer automated diagnosis, risk assessment or access to research records. Describing digital research does not imply approval for every AI application.
