Youth Mental Health Manual

Practical support for the places young people learn and live.

The NEPS manual brings mental-health awareness, everyday support and safe referral into school and community settings. This page describes its scope; the public download awaits release approval. [2]

Support within clear boundaries

Teachers and school staff are not therapists. Their role is awareness, support, observation, safe communication and referral—not diagnosis or clinical treatment.

01

Understand and recognise

Mental-health literacy creates a shared language for wellbeing and distress. The manual considers educational pressure, poverty-related stressors and stigma, with attention to recognising when a young person may need additional support.

  • Mental-health literacy and stigma reduction
  • Early identification of distress without labelling or diagnosis
  • Educational pressure and poverty-related stressors
02

Support and connect

School and community support can make space for safe conversations, everyday coping and supportive relationships. Training and defined role boundaries underpin delivery.

  • Resilience and coping
  • Psychological first aid within trained, non-clinical roles
  • Peer support and teacher support
  • Safe communication and inclusive school environments
03

Protect and refer

Safeguarding and referral are integral to delivery. The public overview does not replace local procedures, trained supervision or a verified referral directory.

  • Safeguarding and child protection
  • Locally verified referral pathways
  • Wellbeing monitoring with consent and appropriate privacy
04

Co-deployment with schools and communities

The proposed model moves from NEPS-led demonstration to joint delivery, followed by school or community-host delivery under supervision and follow-up. It is a proposed implementation design for review, not evidence of universal delivery or amended approval. [3]

Proposed co-deployment sequence [3]

Building shared delivery

  1. 01

    Demonstrate

    NEPS-led demonstration and readiness checks.

  2. 02

    Deliver together

    Joint NEPS–school or community-host facilitation.

  3. 03

    Support local delivery

    Bounded school or community-host delivery under NEPS supervision.

  4. 04

    Follow up and learn

    Review feasibility, acceptability, fidelity and safeguarding.
