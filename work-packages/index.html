Work packages

One programme. Seven connected work packages.

Explore the purpose, activities and planned outputs of each work package. Together, they connect scientific enquiry, youth participation, delivery and accountability.

Programme note

These seven website categories follow the requested public-facing structure. Source documents use varying workstream terminology; the formal WP7 designation and final allocation of responsibilities require consortium confirmation.

WP1WP2WP3WP4WP5WP6WP7
WP 01

Management, Administration and Finance

Coordinate a coherent, accountable multi-country programme.

Key activities

  • Coordinate consortium planning and reporting
  • Support administration, financial stewardship and risk monitoring
  • Connect work package and country delivery plans

Planned outputs

  • Programme plans and reporting
  • Administrative and financial oversight records

Responsible structures: Ms Akosua A. Gyamfuah, Project Manager; Mr Robert Annorhene, MEAL Officer. Formal WP1 lead and finance responsibilities to be confirmed.

WP 02

Psychosocial and Educational Stressors Assessment

Examine educational pressure, poverty-related stressors, stigma and the social context of youth mental health.

Key activities

  • Harmonise psychosocial and educational assessment tools
  • Explore school, family and community experiences
  • Examine resilience, social support and help-seeking

Planned outputs

  • Contextual and psychosocial evidence
  • Harmonised assessment tools and analysis

Responsible structures: Dr Asare Kotor, WP2 Lead. Supporting team responsibilities and local arrangements to be confirmed.

WP 03

Biological and Neurocognitive Studies

Explore biological, epigenetic and neurocognitive dimensions of stress alongside psychosocial evidence.

Key activities

  • Undertake protocol-defined assessments in selected participants
  • Investigate epigenetics in relation to stress and youth mental health
  • Relate biological, epigenetic and cognitive measures to psychosocial experiences
  • Coordinate participant protection and specialist oversight

Planned outputs

  • Biological, epigenetic and neurocognitive research evidence
  • Integrated analyses across research domains

Responsible structures: Prof Obed Brew, WP3 Lead. Supporting team responsibilities and local arrangements to be confirmed.

WP 04

Longitudinal Digital Surveillance

Support repeated research observations to understand changing youth mental-health trajectories.

Key activities

  • Coordinate longitudinal follow-up
  • Support secure digital capture and quality assurance
  • Connect repeated measures across work packages

Planned outputs

  • Longitudinal research evidence
  • Data quality and monitoring outputs

Responsible structures: Dr Linda A Banning, WP4 Lead. Supporting team responsibilities and local arrangements to be confirmed.

WP 05

Policy, Advocacy and Community Engagement

Connect research and lived experience with decisions in education, health and community systems.

Key activities

  • Engage youth, communities and policy stakeholders
  • Synthesise evidence and develop policy briefs
  • Explore implementation, inclusion and financing pathways

Planned outputs

  • Evidence briefs and engagement materials
  • Policy and practice recommendations

Responsible structures: Prof Linda A Fondjo, WP5 Lead. Supporting team responsibilities and local arrangements to be confirmed.

WP 06

Youth-Led Interventions

Develop and learn from culturally relevant support approaches with young people, schools and communities.

Key activities

  • Develop mental-health literacy and support materials
  • Explore shared delivery with schools and community hosts
  • Assess feasibility, acceptability, safeguarding and learning

Planned outputs

  • Refined intervention manuals and training resources
  • Pilot learning and implementation evidence

Responsible structures: Rev. (Prof.) Frances Emily Owusu-Ansah, WP6 Lead. Supporting team responsibilities and local arrangements to be confirmed.

WP 07

Governance, Advisory Boards and Oversight

Provide a clear public account of scientific, community and youth oversight.

Key activities

  • Connect scientific, community and youth advisory perspectives
  • Support accountability and ethical oversight
  • Review programme learning and risks

Planned outputs

  • Advisory recommendations
  • Governance and oversight records

Responsible structures: Programme leadership and advisory structures; public roster and remits to be confirmed.
