Our scientific approach

Seeing the whole picture of youth wellbeing.

NEPS brings psychosocial, biological, epigenetic, neurocognitive and longitudinal research into conversation with young people’s lived experiences. The programme examines how pressures interact with support, resilience and help-seeking. [1]

Young people at the centre

Research that listens. Support that belongs.

The Youth Mental Health Manual and Creative Art Therapy pathway connect mental-health literacy, creative expression and safe support. The proposed school co-deployment model builds shared delivery with NEPS teams and schools. [2–4]

Support and referral are central. Teachers and school staff are not therapists.
